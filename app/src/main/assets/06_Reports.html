Monte Carlo Simulation

What is this? This historical Monte Carlo simulation uses actual annual S&P 500 total returns—including dividends—from 1928 through 2025. It rearranges those real market years into thousands of possible retirement paths, so major gains, crashes, and recovery periods come from history rather than an assumed average return.
