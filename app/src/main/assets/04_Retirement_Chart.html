Before using this retirement chart, please understand the tax tables currently loaded.

  • The Federal tax setup includes selectable 2026 profiles for Married Filing Jointly, Single, Head of Household, Married Filing Separately, and Qualifying Surviving Spouse.

IRA → Roth conversion tax treatment

This chart now models the conversion tax as being paid from the Roth at year-end. The full conversion enters the Roth first, grows during the year, and then the estimated conversion tax is removed from the Roth.
What the chart is currently using
The active model is Roth pays at year-end. That means the full IRA→Roth conversion is deposited into the Roth first, then the estimated extra tax caused by the conversion is removed from the Roth at year-end.
Best choice — Cash / Savings
Use money outside retirement accounts. The full conversion stays in the Roth and can keep growing tax-free. This is usually the cleanest and most efficient option.
Traditional IRA withholding
The custodian withholds the estimated tax before the Roth deposit is completed. Example: convert $10,000, withhold $2,000, and only $8,000 reaches the Roth.
Roth pays at year-end
The full conversion reaches the Roth first. The estimated tax dollars may grow for the year, but then the tax is paid from the Roth. This can look slightly better than IRA withholding in the chart because of timing, but it still reduces tax-free Roth assets.

ⓘ How historical returns affect this chart
When Historical Data is turned on:
The chart uses actual annual S&P 500 total-return percentages for the selected event plus the chosen number of years before and after it. The default is 5 years on each side. Those historical percentages replace the normal Investment Return setting only inside the blue historical-context range.

Event range:
The red or green line identifies the selected crisis, recovery, or boom inside the wider historical context.

Outside the historical context:
The chart returns to the Investment Return percentage selected in your regular chart settings. The complete context window is placed at a random valid location within the retirement timeline. This is a historical stress test, not a prediction of future returns.
